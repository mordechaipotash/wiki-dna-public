# ZMAT -- Zur Mera Asei Tov

Blockchain-based human trust protocol, borrowing from the halachic system of witnesses (eidim).

The org is guided by the Torah it is for every human. it is not for profit rather to pursue good."

ZMAT = Zur Mera Asei Tov (סור מרע ועשה טוב) = "Turn from evil and do good" (Tehillim 34:15). A blockchain-based protocol for human identity verification and trust scoring, borrowing the concept of halachic eidim (witnesses) -- two reliable witnesses who attest to another person's identity and trustworthiness.

The core insight: the halachic concept of eidim (two witnesses required for legal transactions) maps directly to blockchain verification. Human attestation, not biometric or algorithmic, as the trust foundation.

### The Halachic Aidim Principle

> "try find the original zmat convo on blockchain and how we know someone is human 2 ppl have to verify borrowed concept from halachic aidim"
> -- **2025-11** | searching for genesis

The key innovation: in a world of bots, deepfakes, and AI-generated identities, the ancient halachic principle of eidim provides a framework. Two humans who know each other attest to a third. Their attestation reputation is tracked. False witness degrades your trust score. The system is self-correcting because dishonesty is costly.

## Evolution

### 2023 -- Vision Phase

ZMAT started as a nonprofit vision. Domain registered (zmat.org), GitHub repo created (zmat-protocol), Telegram group established. The scope was ambitious: a global trust network.

### 2025 -- Integration with Bottleneck Thesis

> "find how my zmat specifically the idea of hearing blockchain style. Everybody's score is a factor of how accurately they determine if someone else is human and if they good and nobody wants to lie because it will just affect their score negatively"

ZMAT evolved from a standalone project to a component of the larger [[bottleneck-economy|Bottleneck Economy]] thesis. In a world where AI makes identity fraud trivial, human attestation becomes the scarce resource -- another bottleneck that's actually an amplifier.

### 2026 -- The Agent Identity Problem

> "no you are exactly wrong i am saying we 100% need an immutable blockchain of human attestation for sure and which human said which human is human and then find the agent tied to human concept"

With AI agents proliferating, ZMAT's relevance sharpened. The question isn't just "is this person human?" but "which human authorized this agent?" Every AI agent needs a traceable chain back to a verified human -- and that chain needs to be immutable, attestation-based, and halachically-inspired.
