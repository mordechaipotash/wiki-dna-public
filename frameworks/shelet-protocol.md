## Evolution

### The Compression Stack (4 Phases)

```
PHASE 1: CAPTURE     infinity -> 10^6     Reality Crystallization
PHASE 2: COMPRESS    10^6 -> 10^3         Pattern Extraction
PHASE 3: CHOOSE      10^3 -> 1            Sovereignty Point (THE BOTTLENECK)
PHASE 4: EXECUTE     1 -> infinity        AI Scale with Proofs
```

**Phase 1 -- CAPTURE:** Converts infinite physical reality into finite digital artifacts. Voice becomes transcript. Meetings become logs. Nothing is lost yet -- just crystallized.

**Phase 2 -- COMPRESS:** AI extracts structure from chaos. Raw text becomes embeddings, conversations become themes, behavior becomes patterns. Still too much for human working memory.

**Phase 3 -- CHOOSE:** The bottleneck. Compresses thousands of patterns into 2-5 auditable options. Human decides with confidence. Decision is logged, auditable, reversible. 10,000 options = paralysis. 2-5 options = agency. 1 option = AI decided, human rubber-stamped.

**Phase 4 -- EXECUTE:** One human decision triggers infinite AI execution. Every step documented, auditable, reversible.
