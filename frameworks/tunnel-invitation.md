# Tunnel Invitation

Three modes of communicating from inside a monotropic tunnel: Translation, Demonstration, Invitation.

## Definition

> "I feel a tremendous weight lifted off at the idea that I could invite someone into my tunnel as is without having to create the explicit context for them... This concept is life changing"
> -- **2026-03-10** | Discord #general

Tunnel Invitation is a framework for how monotropic thinkers can share their deep focus states with others. Instead of the exhausting work of translating out of the tunnel (losing fidelity) or demonstrating results (losing process), you can invite someone INTO the tunnel -- with AI maintaining the context bridge.

## Genesis

### The Breakthrough: March 10, 2026

The concept emerged in a Discord message where the author articulated something he'd been feeling but hadn't named. The "tremendous weight lifted" was the realization that the exhausting translation work of masking could be bypassed entirely.

Before this moment, the options were:
1. Stay in the tunnel and be misunderstood
2. Leave the tunnel to explain (losing the state)
3. Mask/translate (exhausting, lossy)

The new option: invite them in. Let the AI handle context. Stay in the tunnel. Share the depth.

## Evolution

### The Three Modes

**Mode 1: Translation**
Translate your tunnel-state thinking into neurotypical-accessible language. This is what masking does manually. AI can do it better, but it's still lossy -- you're converting depth into breadth.

**Mode 2: Demonstration**
Show the output of the tunnel. "Look what I built." This preserves some fidelity but loses the process, the thinking, the WHY behind the WHAT.

**Mode 3: Invitation**
Invite the other person INTO your tunnel. The AI maintains context, provides the bridge. The visitor experiences the depth. No translation loss. No masking required.

This is what the author called "life changing" -- the possibility that someone could experience his actual cognitive state without him having to leave it.
