# Cognitive Sovereignty

A personal AI agent layer between human and information environment, ensuring 100% human control over AI-mediated decisions.

## Definition

> "Find my 4 stage physical world to AI step to human action 100 agency to perfect output/dashboard/reports make this concept my vision AI as hyperpersonalised hyperefficiency 100 human"
> -- **2025-08** | Personal Executive Profile Analysis | ChatGPT

Cognitive sovereignty is the principle that every human should maintain full control over their information environment. Not "human in the loop" (human as obstacle), but "AI in the loop" (human as conductor). The AI acts as a prosthetic layer that amplifies human cognition while preserving human agency at every decision point.

## Genesis

The concept emerged from the author's own experience as a monotropic thinker navigating an information-dense world. The problem: infinite information, finite attention, monotropic processing. The solution: a personal AI layer that compresses the world into human-decidable choices.

> "the bottleneck being AI human, human AI translation as 100% human sovereignty assumed"
> -- **2025-12** | intellectual-dna

The word "sovereignty" was chosen deliberately -- not "assistance" or "augmentation" but sovereignty. The human is sovereign. The AI serves the sovereign.
