# HPI -- Hyper-Personalized Intelligence

Algorithms FOR users, not ON users.

## Definition

> "I want each human to own [an API] that sits between them and the world and creates a personalised filter between the world and them based on their preferences."
> -- **2024-04** | Making Organizational Contributions Accessible | ChatGPT

> "hpi is a hyperpersonalised api"
> -- **2025-12** | deep dive session

HPI is a portable, user-owned cognitive API that learns from the individual and mediates between them and the information environment. Unlike current algorithms (which optimize for engagement, ads, or platform metrics), HPI optimizes for the individual human's preferences, goals, and cognitive style.

## Evolution

### The Core Inversion

Current algorithms: Platform owns the model. User is the product. Optimization = engagement/revenue.

HPI: User owns the model. Platform is the tool. Optimization = user's stated preferences and cognitive style.

### Relationship to Brain MCP

the author's Brain MCP (377K messages, 118K embeddings) is a working prototype of HPI -- a personal knowledge layer that knows how he thinks, what he's explored, and what connections matter. The difference: Brain MCP is a developer tool. HPI is the vision for every human.

### The iamwhoiam Connection

The identity layer matters: HPI isn't just preferences, it's cognitive identity. How do you process? What's your attention style? What's your working memory capacity? The API adapts to YOU, not to a demographic average.
