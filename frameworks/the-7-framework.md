# The 7 Framework

Dimensional mental health: HEAD, GUARD, HEART, GUT, GRIP, GROUND, REACH.

## Definition

> "yeah but i want to spend all our time on Phase 1: Self-Assessment Framework -- Define 8-12 core chars (accessible language, not clinical jargon) -- Create self-mapping questions -- Visual representation of current fingerprint first in order to try replace the pathetic legacy 'buckets' how does that work how could this work think with me"
> -- **2026-01** | mental-health session

The 7 Framework replaces categorical psychiatric diagnosis (DSM buckets) with a dimensional, body-mapped, person-first system. Instead of "which disorder do you have?", ask "where are you on each dimension, and where do you want to be?"

Seven questions your body already knows:

| Dimension | Question | Spectrum |
|-----------|----------|----------|
| **HEAD** | Where's my head at? | scattered <--> locked |
| **GUARD** | Is my guard up? | down <--> up |
| **HEART** | Is my heart in it? | numb <--> chasing |
| **GUT** | What's my gut say? | empty <--> overflowing |
| **GRIP** | Am I gripping or letting go? | loose <--> tight |
| **GROUND** | Am I grounded? | floating <--> frozen |
| **REACH** | Am I reaching out or pulling in? | pulled in <--> merged |

## Evolution

### Disorder Mapping

Same characteristics, different combinations. "Disorders" are fingerprints:

| Traditional Bucket | Primary Dimensions |
|-------------------|-------------------|
| Major Depression | HEART down, GUT down, GRIP up, REACH down |
| Anxiety (GAD) | GUARD up, GRIP up, GUT up |
| PTSD | GUARD up, GROUND down, HEART down, REACH down |
| ADHD | HEAD scattered, GRIP down, HEART chasing |
| OCD | HEAD locked, GRIP very up, GUARD up |
| BPD | GROUND very down, REACH up, GRIP chaotic |

Different fingerprints, not different diseases. Two people with "depression" might have completely different fingerprints -- and need different interventions.

### Key Principles

1. No position is inherently "right" -- context determines whether a position serves you
2. Both ends can be problematic -- not "more is better" or "less is better"
3. States, not traits -- this is where you ARE, not who you ARE
4. You define the target -- not a norm, not a professional, you
5. Body already knows -- the framework names what you already feel

### Clinical Application

Instead of: "You have MDD + GAD"

Say: "Your GUARD is elevated, your HEART is muted, your GUT is depleted, and your GRIP is tight. Let's figure out which one to target first and what moves it for you."
