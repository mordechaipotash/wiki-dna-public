# Theory of Mind Prosthetic

An AI-powered system that bridges the communication gap between monotropic and neurotypical minds.

## Definition

> "remember this is a theory of mind prosthetic so each facet needs to match the average readers so it is fake to say never mention xyz since at the end of the day all facets are me and i might want one day to talk to my clients about my autism but it still needs to be in the consistent voice of the clients"
> -- **2026-01** | Clawdbot session

> "theory of mind say it in a way anyone could/would understand"

A Theory of Mind Prosthetic is an AI system that compensates for the communication mismatch between how a monotropic person thinks and how neurotypical people expect to receive information. Not translating DOWN or simplifying -- translating ACROSS. Preserving the depth while making it accessible.

1. **Personal pain** (2023): "I need a prosthetic" -- raw need
2. **AI as prosthetic** (2024): Using AI conversations themselves as the prosthetic -- having AI translate his thinking into accessible language
3. **Brain MCP** (2025): Building a literal cognitive prosthetic -- 377K messages searchable, context recoverable, tunnel state preservable

### The Bidirectional Prosthetic

The theory of mind prosthetic works in two directions:

- **Outward:** Translating monotropic depth into neurotypical-accessible communication
- **Inward:** Injecting context back into the monotropic tunnel when switching is needed
