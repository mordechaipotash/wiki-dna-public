## What It Is

166 curated markdown files published as a GitHub Pages site. "The interpretation layer" -- distilled thinking across domains, formatted for humans and search engines. The public-facing crystallization of patterns extracted from 339K+ conversations.

## Why It Exists

Brain MCP stores everything. Poly-Wiki stores what matters. It bridges the gap between raw conversation archives and publishable intellectual output. Better than Brain for interpretability -- structured, curated, linkable.

> "look in https://github.com/mordechaipotash/poly-wiki this is better than brain for interpretability" (2026-01)
